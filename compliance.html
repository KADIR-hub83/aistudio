Security and SOC2 Governance

Our governance approach is organised around the five SOC 2 trust services criteria: security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality and privacy.

Security: access control, monitoring and incident response

Availability: resilience, backups and recovery

Processing integrity: reviewed, accurate and complete processing

Confidentiality: data classification and encryption

Privacy: collection, use and retention of personal data
