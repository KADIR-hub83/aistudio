Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to help you get started.

How do I enquire about an AI workshop?

Open Contact Us, select “AI Training / Workshop” and describe your requirements, or email support@aibuilder.co.in.

How can my company request an AI program?

Use Contact Us and choose “Corporate AI Program”. Include your organisation, goals and preferred timeline.

How do I contact support on WhatsApp?

Click a WhatsApp support link. It opens a chat with +1 904 921 3470. WhatsApp Web may open if the app is not installed.

Can I contact support by email or phone?

Email support@aibuilder.co.in or call 9008308909. You can also use the WhatsApp link.

Where can I ask a question not listed here?

Send your question through Contact Us or email our support team. Do not include passwords or sensitive account information.
